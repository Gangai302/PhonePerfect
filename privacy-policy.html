Privacy Policy

Application: PhonePerfect (com.phoneperfect.finder)
Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Summary: PhonePerfect is committed to protecting your privacy. We do not require account creation, nor do we sell, rent, or trade your personal data. All device searches, bookmarks, and comparison criteria are stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application PhonePerfect (Package: com.phoneperfect.finder), developed to assist users in comparing smartphone specifications, finding real-time market prices, and discovering phone recommendations.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

PhonePerfect is designed with privacy-by-design principles:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact books, or physical addresses.
  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the application without registering or logging into an account.
  • No Location Tracking: The application does not access, track, or record your precise GPS location.
  • No Financial Data: We do not collect or store credit card, bank details, or payment credentials.

3. Information Collected and Handled

The application only accesses the following minimal information strictly necessary for core functionality:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to fetch live smartphone specifications, price updates from online aggregators (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart), phone catalog images, and AI recommendation responses.
  • Local Storage (on-device): Your saved favorite devices, comparison histories, and search preferences are stored exclusively on your local device storage using standard local storage. We do not transmit your saved favorites to external private servers.
  • Standard Diagnostic & Network Data: Basic technical logs (such as network response status) may be processed in transient memory purely to display connectivity status and handle offline caching.

4. Third-Party Services and External Links

The application may contain affiliate or direct links to third-party e-commerce platforms (such as Amazon and Flipkart) to allow users to view or purchase devices:

  • When clicking external purchase links, you are directed to third-party websites or apps whose privacy practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • We encourage you to review the privacy statements of any third-party websites you visit.

5. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your interactions. All network communication between the application and catalog APIs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS).

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Permissions Disclosures

PhonePerfect requests the minimum permissions required to function:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for retrieving smartphone specifications, pricing updates, and images.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of those changes.
